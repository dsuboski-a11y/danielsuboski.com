About

A broad background, brought to one conversation.

My professional background spans healthcare, paramedicine, military medical service, hospital operations, community mental-health work, adult education, coaching and facilitation.

That mix has taught me to listen for what matters, distinguish signal from noise, and turn difficult situations into clear next actions without pretending there is a one-size-fits-all answer.

My work is educational and coaching-oriented. When medical, psychological, legal or other regulated professional care is needed, I encourage people to work with the appropriate licensed professional.
