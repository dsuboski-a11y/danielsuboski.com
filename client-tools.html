Before a session

  • What is the one issue that would make this session worthwhile?
  • What changed since we last spoke?
  • What have you tried?
  • What are you avoiding deciding, saying or doing?
  • What would useful progress look like by the end of the conversation?

After a session

  • Write the one sentence you do not want to forget.
  • Name the smallest next action.
  • Put that action somewhere it will actually happen.
  • Notice what gets easier or harder when you begin.
