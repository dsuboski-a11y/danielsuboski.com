How value will be counted

Each event needs a description, date, source receipt, attribution to Fortuna, currency, and validation status. Earned revenue requires actual payment evidence. Cost savings require a credible baseline and an avoided expense. Time saved is separate from cash and will not be double-counted.

Value by mission

Filter verified events by BedWars Coach, Fortuna OS, and approved household or business missions. Financial impact, estimated opportunity, and hypothetical value remain distinct categories.
