The promise

What Fortuna is being built to do

Promises from the canonical product/manual are shown as design targets unless the live state proves them complete.

🧠

Remember projects

Durable state, checkpoints and task continuity instead of depending on a chat window or Daniel’s memory.

🛡️

Grow usefulness, not authority

Every state-changing action belongs behind explicit capability policy. Capability may increase; authority does not automatically increase.

⏹️

Stop means stop

A hard STOP belongs outside model reasoning, with PAUSE, restart recovery and duplicate prevention.

📱

Visible from your phone

Status first, then STOP/PAUSE/RUN-NEXT and narrow approvals, without forcing remote desktop for normal supervision.

🎮

Yield to humans

Games and active household use outrank background AI. Fortuna should checkpoint, release resources and resume safely.

🏠

Live throughout the house

Room terminals, voice, displays and permissioned sensors can form a distributed household interface while keeping privacy and authority separate.

Constitutional principle
“A personal AI that grows in usefulness without silently growing in authority.”

Critical irreversible actions require verified identity, verified authority and explicit approval. Cameras, voice recognition or device possession never grant authority by themselves.
