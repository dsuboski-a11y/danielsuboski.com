About Daniel

Healthcare experience. Deep personal work. Practical leadership.

My background spans military medical service, paramedicine, nursing, community healthcare, coaching, facilitation and leadership. I bring both practical experience and a commitment to emotional healing and human connection.

My training includes Accomplishment Coaching's year-long Life Coaching and Leadership Development programs, Whole Hearted Healing through the Institute for the Study of Peak States, Compassion Key and Ancestral Clearing.

Our work together may focus on emotional healing, personal and professional growth, relationships or leadership. We'll discuss which approach fits your goals and what support is appropriate.
